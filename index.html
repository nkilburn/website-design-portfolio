02 / What we do

Two practices,
one studio.

We started in 2021 as a web design outfit. Three years in, half our work is automation: the back-office pipelines, agent scripts, and dashboards that quietly do the work no one wants to do.

The two halves feed each other. A good website without automation behind it leaks leads. A good automation without a clear surface to look at never gets used. We ship both, in the same engagement, by the same small team.

03 / Principles

Three rules we
haven't broken.

01

Fixed fee, fixed scope.

If we underestimate the work, that's our problem — not yours. We've never sent a change order in four years of operation, and we don't plan to start. The fee you sign is the fee you pay.

02

The team stays the same.

The people who write the proposal are the people who write the code. No juniors swapped in after kickoff, no offshore team, no "delivery manager" between you and the people doing the work.

03

Hand off, don't hold hostage.

You get the code, the credentials, the docs, and the option to never talk to us again. Most clients stay on a small retainer — but that's a choice, not a requirement. No vendor lock-in, ever.

04 / The team

Four people,
wearing few hats.

Two of us design and write. Two of us build and ship. We all talk to clients. We all do QA. We all write the docs. The hat count stays low on purpose — it's how the work stays coherent.

We're based in Brooklyn, NY, with one person in Lisbon. We work Eastern US hours, on Pacific time when the client is on the West Coast, and never on weekends.

01Design & strategy

The same person who writes the homepage headline will design the homepage. The same person who designs the dashboard will write the empty states. We don't believe in handing off copy to a designer or design to a developer.

05 / Tools we use

The stack, briefly.

We're tool-agnostic and opinion-poor. We use what your team will maintain. Here's what we default to when starting from scratch.

Design

Figma

For visual design, prototyping, and handoff. We've tried Penpot, Framer, and Sketch — Figma wins.

Web build

Next.js · Webflow · Framer

Next.js for apps, Webflow for marketing sites with non-technical editors, Framer for design-led launches.

Automation

n8n · Make

n8n for anything we'd want to debug, Make for anything we'd want to hand off. Both, depending on the client.

AI

OpenAI · Anthropic

GPT-4o for tool use and structured output, Claude for long-context reasoning, Whisper for transcription.

Data

Postgres · Supabase

Postgres for everything that needs to last, Supabase when we want auth and storage in the same window.

Internal apps

Retool · Next.js

Retool for things only your team will see, Next.js for anything that might eventually become a product.

Comms

Slack · Linear · Notion

Slack for talking, Linear for tracking, Notion for docs. We use your stack if you have one — these are defaults.

Deploy

Vercel · Railway · Cloudflare

Vercel for Next.js, Railway for Python services, Cloudflare for everything else. We avoid AWS unless you're already there.
